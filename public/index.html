? HOW TO PLAY
Slide the left pad to steer. GAS and BRAKE on the right, HAND BRAKE to drift. EXIT to get out and walk (RUN sprints). PARCELS: load up at the Post Office, park near the address, EXIT, TAKE PARCEL at the boot, walk it to the door and DELIVER. Walk to your front door and tap ENTER HOUSE: bed saves, fridge refills stamina, wardrobe changes clothes. The garage bench is your shop for paint and upgrades. Drag anywhere on the screen to look around the camera. Tap outside a panel to close it. Best in landscape.
Keyboard: arrows/WASD · space = handbrake · C = camera · R = reset · E = use · Esc = menu
